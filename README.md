# Nikah Nama columns in English

The 25 numbered columns of the Pakistani Nikah Nama (marriage registration form under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961), with an English rendering of each printed heading and a plain Urdu paraphrase to help match the column on a paper form.

- [`columns.csv`](columns.csv) and [`columns.json`](columns.json): `column`, `english`, `urdu_paraphrase`.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0. Attribution: NikahDraft, https://nikahdraft.appin60.com

The English is our own rendering for translation drafts; provincial forms vary slightly in wording, and the Urdu lines are paraphrases, not the printed text. Check every column against the form in front of you.

## Use it

To turn a filled-in Nikah Nama into an English translation draft in this column order, with dower amounts in words, tola in grams and translator's notes for blank or struck-out columns, use **[NikahDraft](https://nikahdraft.appin60.com)**. It runs in the browser and sends nothing you type to a server.
